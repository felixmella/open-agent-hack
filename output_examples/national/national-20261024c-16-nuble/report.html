What each class means
OutdatedAn older official figure (e.g. the Census 2017 count) shown as the current population.
Different definitionAn official figure under another definition (an INE estimate or projection, or only the urban or rural part of a census count) shown where the comuna's census count is expected. Not an error in itself.
Withdrawn censusAttributed to the 2012 census, whose results INE withdrew.
Nonexistent censusAttributed to a census Chile never held (e.g. 1999, 2022).
Mislabelled vintageA real official figure labelled with the wrong year or census, or cited to a source that doesn't hold it (citation mismatch).
Internal inconsistencyThe article contradicts itself (infobox vs body).
Unexplained mismatchMatches no official figure, and nothing in the sources explains it.
Rank or qualifiersWikidata statements lack the rank, point in time, method or reference needed to use them correctly.
UnsourcedNo reference, or a reference that can't support the figure.
Likely errorA typo, a wrong unit or a corrupted field.
Missing valueThe source has no population figure at all.
